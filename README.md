# MaziwaLog

**Every litre tracked. Every shilling accounted for.**

MaziwaLog is the digital ledger for Kenyan dairy cooperatives — one place to record milk deliveries, run the agrovet credit book, and pay farmers accurately at month-end. It replaces the handwritten passbook with a transparent record that managers, clerks, and farmers can all trust.

---

## Why MaziwaLog?

Collection day moves fast: milk is weighed at the yard, supplies are handed out on credit, and at month-end someone has to work out who is owed what. MaziwaLog keeps those numbers consistent from the dock to the payout, so no one has to re-derive the maths from a stack of paper slips.

- **One ledger for the whole cooler** — intake, credit, and payouts in the same place.
- **Built for the field** — high-contrast tables that stay legible in bright collection-yard light, numpad-friendly entry, and large touch targets.
- **Honest by design** — every entry is attributed, every statement is auditable, and every cooler's data stays strictly its own.

---

## Who it's for

| Role | What they get |
|------|---------------|
| **Managers** | Full view of the cooler: intake volumes, credit owed, month-end payouts, and printable statements. |
| **Clerks** | A fast intake screen for morning and evening weigh-ins, with accepted/rejected litres and quality reasons. |
| **Farmers** | A personal portal showing their litres, credit, and net payout — no more waiting for the month-end notice. |

Each cooler workspace is completely separate: one co-op can never see another's ledger.

---

## What it does

- **Fast milk intake** — morning and evening weigh-ins with accepted/rejected litres and mandatory quality reasons.
- **Agrovet store credit** — supplies charged to an account and deducted automatically at payout time.
- **M-Pesa payouts** — STK Push repayment through the co-op's own Till/Paybill, with live status and a receipt every time.
- **Month-end statements** — litres × rate − credit = a clear, printable net payout each farmer can read and sign off.
- **Role-based access** — manager, clerk, and farmer views kept cleanly apart.
- **Workspace isolation** — every cooler keeps its own data, always.

---

## How it works

1. **Record the delivery** — the clerk logs litres at the yard as the cans come in.
2. **Track credit and earnings** — agrovet supplies and daily intake accumulate against each farmer.
3. **Reconcile and pay** — the manager generates month-end statements and settles payouts over M-Pesa.

The same numbers follow the farmer from the first litre to the final shilling.
