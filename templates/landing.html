{# ===== HOW IT WORKS ===== #}
Process

From the dock to the payout

Three clear steps, same numbers end to end.

01

Record the delivery

Pick the farmer, choose AM or PM session, enter accepted and rejected litres. Rejections require a reason.

02

Track credit and earnings

Store credit charges and M-Pesa repayments post to the same ledger so balances stay current.

03

Reconcile and pay

Generate month-end statements, settle via the co-op's own Till/Paybill, and attach the receipt.
